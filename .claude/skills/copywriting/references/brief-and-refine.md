# Discovery questions, Creative Brief, and Refine checklist

This is the conversation engine. The goal is to pull the best ideas and details out of the writer *before* drafting. Ask conversationally, a few at a time, and push every answer toward specifics (names, numbers, real quotes).

---

## Discovery questions (Phase 2 — the creative core)

Ask these as a conversation, not a form. Skip anything already answered; follow the interesting threads. If the user is stuck on one, offer examples or a first guess they can react to.

**The offer & outcome**
- In one sentence, what is the thing you're selling/promoting?
- What does it actually *do* for the buyer — the real outcome they walk away with? (Push past features to the after-state, and to the deeper why: status, identity, relief, freedom.)
- What's the price / offer structure, and what's included (bonuses, guarantee)?

**The audience (in their words)**
- Who exactly is this for? Describe one specific person.
- How would *they* describe their problem out loud? What words do they use?
- What do they secretly want that they might not say?
- What have they already tried that failed them?
- (If unknown) Want me to mine Reddit / Amazon reviews / forums for their exact language?

**The one big idea / angle**
- If the reader remembers only one thing, what should it be?
- What's the single most compelling, believable, unexpected message here? (Rule of One — we pick ONE and make everything support it.)

**Proof & credibility**
- What proof do you have? Testimonials, numbers, results, case studies, credentials, press, guarantees. (Anything falsifiable and specific.)

**Unique mechanism / differentiation**
- What can you say that a direct competitor literally could not? What's genuinely different about how this works? Does the method have a name (or should we name it)?

**Objections**
- What are the top 2–3 reasons someone would say no or hesitate?

**Voice**
- How does the brand/you actually sound? Paste a paragraph you've written before, or name 1–2 people whose writing voice fits. (Offer to build a short voice profile: 3–5 personality words, formality/energy/directness, do's, don'ts, forbidden words.)

**Also confirm (from Phase 1 if not already set):** asset, channel, audience temperature (cold/warm/hot), the one CTA, tone, and any length or format constraints.

## Creative Brief template (Phase 3 — confirm before drafting)

```
COPY BRIEF — [asset], for [channel]
Audience:            [one specific person] · Temperature: [cold/warm/hot] · Awareness: [un/problem/solution/product/most-aware]
The ONE big idea:    [single message everything supports]
Primary emotion:     [toward pleasure  |  away from pain]
Promise / outcome:   [the after-state the reader gets]
Unique mechanism:    [what only we can say / named method]
Proof:               [testimonials, numbers, credentials, guarantee]
Top objections:      [1–3, + how we'll answer each]
The one CTA:         [single action] → [where it goes]
Voice:               [personality words; do's / don'ts / forbidden words]
Structure:           [chosen skeleton from playbook, matched to temperature]
Constraints:         [length, format, must-includes, compliance limits]
```

Show it, get a thumbs-up or edits, then draft.

## Refine checklist (Phase 5 — never ship the first draft)

Run the draft through all of these:

- [ ] **Hook earns the read** — understood in ~2 seconds; first line opens a loop (no pleasantries); each line pulls to the next.
- [ ] **3-rule test on key lines** — can I visualize it? falsify it? can nobody else say it? (Fix any line that's three no's.)
- [ ] **4 foundations** — every important line is Specific, Emotional, Memorable, Positioned.
- [ ] **Benefits over features** — every feature is cast as "[feature] so you can [benefit]"; facts pushed to promises.
- [ ] **One idea, one CTA** — nothing competes with the spear tip; one clear action with an obvious control; killed every needless "and."
- [ ] **Concrete & proven** — specifics and facts instead of adjectives; proof near every big claim; objections handled.
- [ ] **Simple & human** — short sentences/paragraphs, everyday words, line-by-line; **read it aloud** and fix every clunk/boring stretch; below ~grade-7 reading level.
- [ ] **Kaplan's Law** — cut any word not actively working; a paragraph should break if you pull a sentence out.
- [ ] **Voice match** — sounds like the brand/person, not generic; matches the voice profile.
- [ ] **No AI slop** — none of the tells below; passes as human-written.
- [ ] **Variations** — offered 2–3 options for the headline and CTA; noted what to A/B test.

---

## AI-slop blacklist (cut these tells)

Words/phrases that scream AI or filler — remove or replace:
`unlock`, `unleash`, `discover`, `dive in`, `delve`, `elevate`, `supercharge`, `harness`, `transform your [X]`, `in today's fast-paced world`, `here's the truth`, `it's not just [X], it's [Y]`, `look no further`, `game-changer`, `revolutionary`, `seamless`, `robust`, `leverage` (as a verb), `navigate the landscape`, `tapestry`, `testament to`, `at the end of the day`, `whether you're a … or a …`.

Also: over-polished symmetry, three-item lists everywhere, and the **em-dash tic** used as a rhythm crutch. Apply the read-aloud test — if a human wouldn't say it that way, rewrite it. Swap in details only the client would know.
